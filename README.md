# adEarly
sMRI based early stage diagnosis of Alzheimer's Disease using longitudinal data

Trying to understand biomarkers that can determine Alzheimer's Disease early on using a computational/machine learning lens. 


Data Acquisition:

1) Data acquired through IDA via an ADNI license. Filtered by 3-D, T-1 weighted MRI images for patients who have visits in the range of 6 months to 72 months [longitudinal data].
2) Patient metdata from IDA for CDR/MMSE scores along with patient demographics and medical history.
